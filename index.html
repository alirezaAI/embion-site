03 / PERCEPTION RESEARCH

From measurement
to meaning.

01 / GEOMETRY

Task-oriented spatial models

Combine visual semantics and sparse range measurements to explore useful obstacle and free-space representations.

02 / MOTION

Radar-like motion cues

Investigate relative range changes over time alongside visual object tracking.

03 / DEPTH

LiDAR-guided depth

Explore using measured scan layers as anchors for stereo depth estimation.

04 / AUDIO

Acoustic-visual awareness

Correlate estimated sound direction with visual events to reason about activity beyond a camera alone.

These are research directions, not certified performance or safety claims.
